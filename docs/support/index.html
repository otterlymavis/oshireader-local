OshiReader Support

Last updated: 2026-09-23

Feed not updating

Pull down on the Feed screen to force a manual refresh. Background refresh runs on a schedule set by iOS and may be delayed if the app hasn't been opened recently, Low Power Mode is on, or Background App Refresh is disabled for OshiReader in iOS Settings > General > Background App Refresh.

Not getting notifications

Check that notifications are enabled for OshiReader in iOS Settings > Notifications, and that the watch term has alerts turned on inside the app (term settings > Notify on New). New items older than a few days are not notified on to avoid a backlog burst.

Paid hosted push tier

The optional paid tier adds near-real-time push for a limited number of watch terms. If push stops arriving, open Settings > Paid Backend and check the status label and term count; use Restore Purchases if a prior purchase isn't recognized after reinstalling.

Backup and restore

Settings > Backup lets you export an encrypted backup file and restore it later, including on a new device. Keep the backup password somewhere safe — it cannot be recovered.

Something else

See the Privacy Policy for what data the app stores and sends. For anything not covered here, please use the app's App Store listing to reach the developer.
