OshiReader is a feed reader for Japanese media topics. The free tier works
entirely on-device with no account or sign-in: it polls public RSS/JSON feeds
and light HTML scrapes directly from the phone on a schedule you control
(foreground pull-to-refresh plus periodic background refresh).

To test the free tier:
1. Tap '+' to add a watch topic (for example: "乃木坂46" or "Anime").
2. Pull down on the Feed screen to refresh and fetch matching items.
3. Tap on any item to open the in-app reader.

Optional paid tier (in-app purchase):
Settings > Paid Backend offers an optional hosted push subscription
(auto-renewing monthly/yearly, or a non-consumable one-time plan) that adds
near-real-time push notifications for a small number of watch terms via our
backend. It is entirely additive — the free on-device feed keeps working
whether or not this is purchased, and no account/sign-in is required for it
either (entitlement is tied to the App Store purchase via StoreKit).
To test: open Settings > Paid Backend, and use a Sandbox Apple ID to purchase
one of the listed products; the "Real-time push terms" counter and status
label update once the purchase completes. "Restore Purchases" is available on
the same screen.

Privacy Policy is available in-app under Settings > Privacy Policy, and is
also hosted at https://otterlymavis.github.io/oshireader-local/privacy/.
